# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Knowledge workers using Logseq to capture connected notes, collaboration context, and actionable tasks during focused daily work. They need fast authoring, trustworthy retrieval, and low-friction task management without leaving their notes.

## Product Purpose

Logseq is a local-first knowledge and task workspace. This fork extends its native task workflow with compact priority controls, scheduled reminders, automatic backups, and completed-task organization. Success means active work remains easy to scan while completed work stays accessible in its original context.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, and trustworthy. New behavior should feel native to Logseq rather than like a separate task application.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard patterns, oversized cards, celebratory completion effects, intrusive modal workflows, and dense project-management chrome. Do not hide completed work permanently or separate it from its note context.

## Design Principles

1. Keep active work visually dominant while preserving completed context.
2. Prefer compact, inline controls over modal configuration.
3. Make one semantic operation behave consistently from the UI, CLI, and agents.
4. Preserve local-first ownership and avoid exposing private graph data.
5. Extend established Logseq interaction and visual patterns rather than inventing parallel ones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Logseq keyboard navigation, focus behavior, contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Status must not be communicated by color alone, and collapsed sections must remain keyboard-operable and clearly labeled.
